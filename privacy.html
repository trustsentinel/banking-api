Privacy Policy

banking-api · Last updated 6 November 2026

Who is responsible

This application, banking-api, is operated by trustsentinel.eu (info@zgos.eu) as the data controller. It is a private, single-user tool: the only person whose data it processes is its own operator. It is not offered to the public and has no other users.

What the application does

banking-api reads the operator's own bank account information in order to reconcile pending payment notices received by email against the charges that actually appear on the account. It is strictly read-only. It cannot initiate payments, move money, or modify anything at the bank.

Access to bank data is provided by Enable Banking Oy, a regulated account information service provider (AISP), acting under the PSD2 framework. The application never holds bank credentials: authentication happens at the bank, and the bank returns an access token tied to an explicit consent.

What data is processed

  • Account identifiers: account name, IBAN, currency, account type.
  • Balances.
  • Transactions: date, amount, currency, status, counterparty name, and payment reference or concept.
  • Technical data needed to maintain the connection: the Enable Banking session identifier and the consent expiry date.

No bank credentials, card numbers, or authentication factors are ever received or stored by this application.

Purpose and legal basis

The sole purpose is personal finance reconciliation for the operator's own accounts. The legal basis is the operator's explicit consent (GDPR Article 6(1)(a)), given to their bank through the Enable Banking authorisation flow and renewable at the operator's choice.

Where the data is stored

Account and transaction data is stored locally, on the operator's own computer, in a file readable only by the operator's user account. Nothing is uploaded to a server operated by this application, because this application has no server. The signing key used to authenticate against the Enable Banking API also never leaves that machine.

Retention

Transactions are cached locally for the period the operator queries, and the cache is refreshed or discarded as it is superseded. The local data store can be deleted at any time by removing the application's data directory, which erases all cached account and transaction data immediately.

Sharing

No data is shared with anyone. There are no third-party recipients, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking, and no transfers outside the EEA performed by this application. Enable Banking Oy processes the data in its role as the regulated provider of the connection, under its own privacy policy.

Automated decisions

The application matches expected charges against transactions by comparing merchant names and amounts. This is a convenience that produces a suggestion on a personal dashboard. No decision with legal or similarly significant effects is made automatically.

Rights

As the only data subject is the operator, rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability and objection are exercised directly by deleting the local data or revoking the consent. Consent can be withdrawn at any time, with immediate effect, in either of two places:

  • In the bank's own application, under third-party or connected-app access.
  • In the Enable Banking control panel, by deleting the session.

Withdrawal stops all further access. Complaints may be addressed to the Spanish data protection authority (AEPD, aepd.es).

Security

The private key used to sign API requests is held locally with owner-only file permissions. Connections to the Enable Banking API use TLS. The application exposes no network service and no write operations of any kind.

Contact

For any question about this policy or the data it describes: info@zgos.eu.
