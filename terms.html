Scope

banking-api is a private tool operated by trustsentinel.eu for that operator's own use. It is not a public service, it has no other users, and no account can be created on it. These terms govern the operator's use of the tool and describe what it does, for the benefit of the account servicing institutions and the account information service provider through which it connects.

What the service does

The application connects to the operator's own payment accounts through Enable Banking Oy, a regulated account information service provider, and retrieves account details, balances and transactions. It uses that information to show which pending payment notices correspond to charges that have already been made.

What the service does not do

  • It does not initiate payments or transfers. It holds no payment initiation functionality of any kind.
  • It does not ask for, receive, or store bank credentials or authentication factors.
  • It does not share data with third parties, sell data, or profile anyone.
  • It does not act on behalf of anyone other than its own operator.

Consent and access

Access requires the operator's explicit consent, granted at the bank through strong customer authentication. Consent is granted for a limited period, after which access stops until it is renewed through the same authenticated flow. The operator may revoke consent at any time from the bank's own application or from the Enable Banking control panel; revocation takes effect immediately.

Access frequency respects the limits set by the account servicing institution and by PSD2, including the limit on unattended access requests per day. The application caches retrieved data locally in order to stay within those limits.

Accuracy

Information shown is a convenience copy of data provided by the bank and may be delayed, incomplete, or superseded. The reconciliation between notices and charges is a suggestion based on matching merchant names and amounts; it can produce false matches or miss genuine ones. The bank's own statements are the authoritative record and should be relied upon for any financial decision.
